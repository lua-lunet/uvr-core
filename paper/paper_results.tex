\begin{abstract}
This document is an addendum to \emph{Diskless Viewstamped Replication with
Unbounded Crash-Stop Reincarnation}; the parent paper's abstract and keywords
are not repeated here. This companion reports only measured and checked
findings, each with its observed numbers, its evidence path in the repository
and the command that reproduces it. It adds no method and changes nothing in
the parent paper or in the proof-ladder addendum; where the repository records
no measurement, the experiment's contract is stated and its findings are
marked \tobemeasured.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope of this report}
Every entry below is a result the repository evidence records: a model-check
log, a kernel-checked proof transcript, a checker's summarised counts, a dated
audit, or a merge record. No experiment was executed for this report, and no
number is inferred, extrapolated or invented. Each finding carries its
provenance path and, in the appendix, the command that reproduces it.

The evidence classes are: TLC model checking of the era and reincarnation
design models (Section~\ref{sec:modelchecking}); mutation and negative
controls, in TLA+, in the Lean mutation harness and inside the proof-ladder
rungs (Section~\ref{sec:mutations}); the Showboat-verified rung transcripts
13--28 (Section~\ref{sec:rungs}); the independent exhaustive checker's counts
for weighted reachability and the fused batch (Section~\ref{sec:exhaustive});
the dated Lean axiom audits (Section~\ref{sec:axioms}); and the stress
bench, the Zig twin and the Maelstrom harness (Section~\ref{sec:bench}).

One intended measurement has no recorded numbers anywhere in the repository
and is marked \tobemeasured{} throughout: the stress-bench lane figures
beyond those preserved in the merge record
(Section~\ref{sec:bench}). This report stands alone; the parent paper
\cite{parent} supplies the method and the design, and the proof-ladder
addendum \cite{ladder} supplies the mathematical statements that the
transcripts below check.

The reincarnation model's invariant set is recorded in
\path{formal/README.md}: \code{FrownChain} (every committed quorum is a strict
majority and consecutive committed eras overlap), \code{EvictedNeverVoter},
\code{MassRule} (at most mass one per node per committed era),
\code{NoStandbyVote}, \code{RebornAfterFence}, \code{SequenceCompletes},
\code{LeaderIsVoter}, and the liveness property \code{Completes}. The stall
witness is the intentionally failing run: with three deaths, two live voters
hold weight 2 of 4, no strict majority exists, and TLC reports \code{Completes}
violated by a trace that ends stuttering in the stalled state --- the recorded
existence proof that the model stalls rather than committing without a
majority.

Two discrepancies between records are noted rather than hidden. First,
\path{formal/README.md} records \path{VrrCoreEras.cfg} as
``837{,}204 distinct / 3{,}358{,}487 generated, depth 32, 3m24s'', while the
archived log records 837{,}204 distinct / 2{,}743{,}933 generated, depth 32,

the generated totals and elapsed times differ between the two records, and
this report quotes the archived
log. Second, \path{formal/README.md} additionally records an era-model campaign
whose raw logs are not archived under \path{evidence/tlc/}: the even-split
four-node exhaustive run (green, 290{,}474 distinct / 1{,}571{,}856 generated,
depth 30), the fixed-seed simulation runs (10{,}000 depth-40 crash traces,
900{,}119 checked states; and 100{,}000 depth-60 traces, seed 1,
26{,}793{,}084 checked states, 1\,h56\,m), the two intentionally false
witness invariants (reached in 207 and 125 distinct states), the
no-pivot-guard experiment (green, 837{,}204 distinct states), and the era
mutations M2, M4, M5 (\code{CommittedLogsAgree}) and M7, M8 (refused by the
named startup gates, zero states). Their provenance is
\path{formal/README.md} and their reproduction commands are the
\code{make tla} family in the appendix.

\section{Mutation controls}\label{sec:mutations}
Three independent control suites are recorded; each rejection is a checked
result, not a review observation.

\subsection{The Lean mutation harness}
\path{formal/uvrr-lean/check_mutations.py} compiles temporary mutants and
requires Lean to reject each. Its recorded design and outcome
(the proof-ladder addendum's appendix states the suite's five rejections and
the hole detection):
an existential quorum checker (\code{Q2.all} replaced by \code{Q2.any}); an
unsafe four-node decision family (the 3-of-4 subsets replaced by
\code{[[3]]}); an omitted next-slot guard in \code{NormalLog}; an append
after the view-change fence in \code{ViewFence}; and a cross-view prepare
receipt in \code{LogProvenance}. The suite additionally checks that a
\code{sorry} is detected through \code{sorryAx} in the axiom query even when
the compiler exits zero. Reproduction: \code{python3 check\_mutations.py} from
\path{formal/uvrr-lean}.

\subsection{The TLC defect regressions}
The nine expected rejections of Table~\ref{tab:tlcred} (one era-model mutant,
five three-node mutants, two five-node mutants, one stall witness) each
enable exactly one weakened perimeter through the \code{Defect} constant and
must exit nonzero on the named invariant; the configuration-to-obligation
mapping is recorded in \path{formal/README.md}, and the \code{make
tla-mutations} lane requires both the failure and the exact violated-invariant
text. The era-model mutations M2, M4, M5, M7 and M8 are recorded in
\path{formal/README.md} as above.

\subsection{Negative controls inside the rungs}
The rung transcripts record their own controls alongside the verified
results: rung 13's length-only selector loses a newer committed prefix and an
omitted scalar length bound misranks reports; rung 14's authentic
out-of-order delivery witnesses failure when the slot guard is bypassed;
rung 15's concrete post-fence append breaks the historical report; rung 16's
delayed wrong-view prepare control checks the model interface; rung 23 states
the honest three-node degenerate negative (the forming quorums coincide, so
no pivot exists and none is exercised); rung 24's casting-vote verdict splits
by boundary (degenerate across E0/E1, genuine pivot across E1/E2); rung 26's
premature promotion of the replacement (claiming weight one in the
intermediate era) is rejected by Lean with an application type mismatch; and
rung 28's hand-written \code{swap\_control} kernel-checks that the
equal-total swap $(1,2,1,2)\to(2,1,2,1)$ makes $\{B,D\}$ a majority at $E_0$
and a minority at $E_1$ --- the preservation hypothesis of the telescoping
theorem is not free. Rung 27's transcript additionally records that both
Leanstral generation rounds for \code{Fuse.lean} failed and that the accepted
proof bodies were repaired locally: the successful result is the repaired
file, not an unassisted generator success.

\section{Exhaustive checker coverage}\label{sec:exhaustive}
The independent Python checker
(\path{study/weighted-reachability/check.py}, standard library only)
records its exact coverage counts in
\path{study/weighted-reachability/generated/summary.json}, and the
repository's written record states the headline totals
(\path{study/weighted-reachability/README.md}).

\subsection{Weighted reachability}
Every weight vector and availability count with a positive live leader
through ten existing identities: 310{,}007 symmetry-representative profiles,
covering 14{,}541{,}815 labelled weight/availability profiles, with zero
failed checks. The maximum phantom weight added is 3 (matching the proved
bound $\delta\leq 3$), and the maximum hub path is 19 unit edits. Every
source/target pair satisfying the premises through six identities:
774{,}159 representative pairs, covering 3{,}939{,}756 ordered labelled pairs,
zero failed checks, every constructed path shortest in unit edits (maximum
path 11). The per-identity breakdown is preserved in
\path{generated/summary.json} and rendered in \path{generated/report.html}.
The same summary records 250 independent cross-checks of the powerset
intersection rule, and separate negative controls that reject an unsafe
two-vote jump, a missing live majority, excessive phantom weight, and
counting the abstract companion as a received quorum.

\subsection{The fused batch}
\path{study/weighted-reachability/check_fuse.py} records
(\path{generated/fuse-summary.json}): the three-voter fused schedule checks
2 boundaries and 64 cross-quorum majority pairs; the five-voter schedule
checks 6 boundaries and 5{,}760 pairs; over four identities with weights in
$\{0,1,2\}$, all 29{,}690 common-response endpoint pairs admit a
monotone unit-edit path (99{,}352 unit edges) that keeps a responding
majority at every step and is shortest; and the negative control walks the
equal-total swap $(1,2,1,2)\to(2,1,2,1)$ through unit edits, recording
responder masses $4,4,3,3,2$ and quorum flags
true, true, false, false, false --- the responding quorum is not preserved
along a legal unit-step path.

Reproduction for both checkers is in the appendix. These finite counts are
computational evidence; the general claims rest on the Lean proofs and the
mathematical note recorded in the same directory.

then \code{python3 check\_axioms.py} from \path{formal/uvrr-lean}.

\section{Stress bench, Zig twin and Maelstrom harness}\label{sec:bench}
\subsection{The Maelstrom stress bench}
The bench's contract is the \code{make} lane set in the \path{Makefile}: the
Maelstrom lin-kv workload against five nodes ($f=2$), 180-second runs at
rate 20 with nemesis interval 10, one lane per fault profile ---
\code{test-clean} (no faults), \code{test-partition},
\code{test-kill}, and \code{test-resurrect} (the kill nemesis against
persisted nodes, where every kill reopens with no stopped quorum and the boot
bumps the identity, a T3 reincarnation). The linearizability verdict is
Maelstrom's own Knossos check; the adapter tests
(\path{tests/maelstrom\_adapter.rs}) assert the node's end of the transport
against a scripted relay, and the recorded note is that the vendored harness
is SIGKILL-only, so no clean-stop lane exists --- the T1--T2 clean-stop
discipline is pinned by the adapter test
\path{tests/maelstrom_adapter.rs} case
\path{a_clean_shutoff_reopens_under_the_same_identity}.

The recorded lane results are preserved in the merge record of the stress
bench's restoration (commit \code{b0bf2f9}, 14 September 2026, and its
landed twin \code{44da48e}): ``Lanes: test-clean 0.7407, test-partition
0.2415/0.2710, test-kill 0.5813, test-resurrect 19 dirty T3 bumps --- all
\code{:valid? :true}.'' Every lane's linearizability check passed; the
resurrection lane recorded 19 dirty T3 identity bumps across its kills. The
decimal figures are recorded in the merge message without further
elaboration, and the raw Maelstrom run artefacts are not committed (the
store and state directories are gitignored); the merge message is the
recorded provenance. Lane figures beyond that record are \tobemeasured.

\subsection{The Zig twin}
The vendored TigerBeetle 0.17.9 storage stack (\path{zig/}, every patched
line recorded in \path{zig/PATCH\_MANIFEST.md}) drives the marker transition
machine over real direct-IO paths, compiled with the pinned Zig 0.14.1. The
recorded outcome (the same merge records): 19 of 19 Zig tests pass with the
pinned toolchain, including the exhaustive $4^4$ marker-assignment test over
the pure machine noted in \path{zig/README.md}. The demo consumer is
\path{examples/uvrr-reincarnation/} (feature \code{uvrr}), which runs the
T1/T2/T3 transitions and membership replay over the vendored IO.

\subsection{The Rust corpus gate}
The rung 28 transcript records the corpus gate at its date:
\code{cargo test --features maelstrom} green, 33 suites, and no inline test
modules under \path{src/}. The repository gate (\code{make check}) adds the
format check, clippy at \code{-D warnings}, and the \code{cli}-featured run.
